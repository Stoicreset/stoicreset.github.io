Privacy Policy

Stoic Reset Studio · Effective 9 November 2026

Last updated 9 November 2026

What we collect

  • From TikTok, after you sign in: your basic profile (display name, avatar and account identifier), the access tokens TikTok issues so the app can post on your behalf, and the view, like, comment and share counts for videos the app posted.
  • From Instagram, after you sign in: your account identifier and username, the access token Instagram issues, and the results of posts the app published.
  • Your own content: the scripts, captions and videos you create and approve in the app.

We do not collect information about other TikTok or Instagram users, followers or viewers beyond the total counts above.

How we use it

  • To post the videos you approve, at the times you schedule.
  • To show you how your posts perform, so you can choose better topics and posting times.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored on the account owner's own private computer and private network storage. Access tokens are kept in a settings file readable only by the app's service account and the computer's administrators. Nothing is stored on public servers. Finished videos are uploaded directly to TikTok or Instagram when they post; the app's computers are never opened to the public internet. This public website contains only these pages.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. Data goes only to TikTok or Instagram when the app posts for you, and script text goes to the Anthropic API to draft and research scripts. No TikTok or Instagram account data is sent to Anthropic.

How long we keep it

Access tokens are kept until you disconnect the app. Post results are kept for as long as you use the app. When you disconnect, tokens are deleted.

Your choices

  • Disconnect at any time from TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) or Instagram (Settings → Apps and websites).
  • Ask for your data to be deleted using the contact below.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.

Contact

Questions about these terms or your data: send a direct message to the Stoic Reset account on TikTok or Instagram.
