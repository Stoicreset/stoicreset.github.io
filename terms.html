What the app is

Stoic Reset Studio is a private publishing tool operated by Stoic Reset. It plans, prepares and schedules short videos about Stoic philosophy and, with the account owner's permission, posts them to the Stoic Reset accounts on TikTok and Instagram. It is used only by the Stoic Reset account owner and is not offered to the public.

The app connects to TikTok or Instagram only after the account owner signs in through that platform's own login screen and approves access. The app never asks for or stores your password. You can disconnect it at any time from your TikTok or Instagram account settings.

Content
